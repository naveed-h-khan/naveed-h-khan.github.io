About

Software engineer based in Bangalore with over a decade of experience building e-commerce, accounting, inventory, and subscription-based SaaS systems using .NET, MS SQL, and PostgreSQL.

My career began in a manufacturing environment during a transition from manual to computerized operations. This shaped my focus on usability, clarity, and designing software around real-world workflows rather than abstractions.

Over the years, I’ve worked across multiple technology stacks and domains, delivering systems that are reliable, maintainable, and used in production for many years.

Experience

I have extensive experience designing and developing business-critical software across manufacturing, accounting, education, and retail distribution domains. My work focuses on building integrated systems that solve operational problems reliably at scale.

Early in my career, I designed and developed an inventory and store management system for a manufacturing organization. The solution handled department-level material indents, in-house stock issues, automatic conversion of shortages into purchase indents, purchase order generation, goods inward processing, and final issuance against original requests.

The system enforced minimum stock levels with automated alerts and was integrated with production planning and control modules to support Bill of Materials (BOM) management and accurate production cost calculations.

I later built full-fledged accounting and inventory systems providing end-to-end bookkeeping, ledger management, and transactional accounting. These included a GST module that automated tax calculations, input tax credit adjustments, and statutory reporting, significantly easing compliance.

In the education domain, I designed and implemented an academic management system combining Student Information System (SIS) and Learning Management System (LMS) capabilities. The platform was deployed at reputed institutions including IIM Ahmedabad and IIM Calcutta.

At IIM Calcutta, I also planned, designed, and implemented a biometric attendance system integrated with student timetables. This became the first biometric-based student timetable and attendance system of its kind in India.

Currently, I work at Logic Soft Pvt. Ltd. , an organization specializing in book distribution systems with decades of domain expertise. The solutions are used by major bookstores in India and abroad to manage warehouses, retail outlets, book fairs, and online sales through e-commerce platforms.
