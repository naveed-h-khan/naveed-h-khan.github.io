About

Software engineer based in Bangalore with close to two decades of hands-on experience building e-commerce, accounting, inventory, and subscription-based SaaS systems using .NET, MS SQL, and PostgreSQL.

I began my career in an environment where software was closely tied to day-to-day business operations, working directly with users and real workflows. This shaped my focus on usability, clarity, and practical system design.

Over the years, I’ve continuously adapted to evolving technologies and platforms, delivering systems that remain reliable, maintainable, and relevant in long-term production use.

Experience

I have extensive experience designing and developing business-critical software across manufacturing, accounting, education, and retail distribution domains, with a strong focus on integrated, scalable systems.

I designed and developed inventory and store management systems covering material indents, stock issuance, purchase workflows, goods inward processing, minimum stock alerts, and production planning with BOM-based costing.

I later built full-fledged accounting and inventory platforms including bookkeeping, ledger management, GST automation, input tax credit adjustments, and statutory reporting.

In the education domain, I developed academic management systems (SIS + LMS) deployed at institutions such as IIM Ahmedabad and IIM Calcutta, including India’s first biometric-based timetable and attendance system.

Currently, I work at Logic Soft Pvt. Ltd. , a Chennai-based company specializing in software solutions for the book trade industry. I contribute to web-based products such as e-commerce platforms and fair management systems supporting large-scale retail and distribution operations.
